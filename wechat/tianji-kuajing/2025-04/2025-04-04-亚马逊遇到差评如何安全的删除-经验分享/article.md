## 一、亚马逊买家评论政策核心要点

1. **评论真实性要求**

- 卖家或关联方（员工、家人）发布虚假评论。
- 通过补偿（金钱、折扣、退款等）换取好评或删除差评。
- 利用第三方服务（如评论俱乐部）操控评论。
- 创建变体商品以聚集评论提升星级。
- 向竞争对手商品发布恶意差评。

- 评论必须真实反映买家对商品的体验，禁止任何形式的操纵行为。

2. **违规后果**

- 提供解决方案（如退款、换货）并承诺改进。

## 三、可删除评论的常见类型

1. **内容违规**：

- 含辱骂、歧视、色情内容。
- 泄露个人信息（如订单号、联系方式）。
- 包含外部链接或广告。

2. **行为违规**：

- 同一用户多次发布相似差评。
- 评论聚焦于物流/卖家服务（非产品本身）。
- 疑似竞争对手操控账户发布恶意差评。

3. **技术性违规**：

- 使用非支持的语言撰写评论。
- 包含乱码、符号图片或重复文本。

## 四、关键注意事项

1. **禁止行为**：

- 直接联系买家要求删评或提供补偿。
- 使用小号刷好评或举报竞争对手。
- 通过变体合并操控评论。

2. **风险提示**：

- 亚马逊对评论操纵零容忍，违规可能导致账户永久封禁。
- 举报需提供充分证据（如评论链接、异常行为记录）。

Amazon's Review Removal Policy: Navigating Fairness in Feedback

A review may be tough, yet it can still be just. Conversely, you might encounter a few generic reviews that are unsuitable. So, how do you distinguish these reviews? Amazon's review removal policy is designed to guide you in this process. Here's what the policy entails:

The review should focus on the product itself, not the seller or any other subject.

Reviews that discuss prices, alternative options, or product availability are not permitted by Amazon.

The content of the review must not be defamatory, harassing, threatening, or inflammatory. In other words, it should not include profanity or hate speech.

Content that promotes nudity or is obscene is not allowed.

Reviews that provide personal contact information or email addresses are prohibited.

Promoting other products or website URLs is not acceptable.

Requesting or offering compensation in exchange for content is forbidden.

Reviews from competitors are not allowed.

Multiple negative reviews by the same customer for a single product are not permitted.
