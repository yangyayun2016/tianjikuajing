**Title: AITA for telling my roommate she should pay a "cat cuddling fee" after she demanded I pay a "smell fee"?**

I (26F) live with my roommate "Jane" (25F) and my cat, "Mochi." When we agreed to move in, Jane was super excited about Mochi. 

For the last month, everything has been honestly great. Jane and Mochi are super close. She's always playing with him, giving him cuddles, and she even started buying him his favorite treats (she asked me if it was okay first, which I thought was really respectful). I genuinely thought I'd hit the jackpot for a roommate.

The problem started last week. She began complaining about a "cat smell."

I'm a clean freak. I was already vacuuming constantly. But to fix this "problem" and keep the peace, I spent a lot of money on a PetSnowy self-cleaning litter box. It auto-cleans, has odor control, the works. Our apartment has never smelled cleaner. I thought this would be the end of it.

I was wrong.Last night, Jane sat me down and said she still thinks the apartment smells and that she feels it's only fair I pay an extra $50 a month as a pet smell fee.

I just... snapped. I was so pissed off. I told her that was insane. And then I said If I have to pay a smell fee for a smell that isn't there, then maybe you should pay a 'cat cuddling fee' for all the time you spend with Mochi.

Well, she absolutely blew up. She called me incredibly petty, childish, and a massive asshole.

We’re not talking to each other right now. Am I wrong ? I'm genuinely conflicted. AITA?
